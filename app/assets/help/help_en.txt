## Quick Start

1. Create a profile (Settings > User Profile)
2. Turn on your MindWave headset
3. Pair it in system Bluetooth settings
4. Go to Session tab. The button next to Start
   shows the length (10m, 1h, ∞ = no timer, P =
   a program): tap it to pick 5, 10, 15, 20 or
   30 min, 1 h, 1 h 30 min, 2 h, Free or
   Programs…, then press Start
5. The app auto-detects and connects
6. Meditate! Audio feedback guides you
7. When your shamatha score holds at or above
   your threshold, the status (top right) lights
   up as a green "SHAMATHA" badge
8. Time above threshold (>Thresh in the live
   stats) and the streak (Streak) count the
   metric that drives the sound (Settings >
   Threshold > Audio control metric), marked » in
   the graph legend when that line is shown. It
   can't change while a session runs (a program
   uses its segments' metrics instead)
9. Moving the threshold during a session changes
   it once the slider rests for half a second:
   the sound, the stats and the SHAMATHA badge
   follow it, and the graph's dashed line steps
   there. In a program the segments set the
   targets: the threshold is locked until it
   stops

## Connection Troubleshooting

If the device won't connect:

1. Check battery — the #1 cause of connection problems.
   Replace the AAA battery if the headset connects
   but the app says "No EEG data received". Use
   NiMH rechargeable (1.2V) and ignore the red LED
   (calibrated for 1.5V alkaline). Avoid Li-ion
   1.5V rechargeable batteries.
2. Check Bluetooth — enabled on phone, headset paired
3. Clean skin and sensors — wash forehead with soap,
   wipe sensor pads with alcohol pad
4. Reset pairing — remove device from BT settings,
   re-pair it (fixes most connection issues)
5. Close other BT apps — only one app can
   hold the RFCOMM connection at a time
6. Restart headset — turn off, wait 5s, turn on
7. "No EEG data received" after connecting —
   weak battery.
   BT radio works on less power than the EEG chip.
   Replace the battery.
8. Test with manufacturer's app — if it also
   can't connect, the headset may be faulty

## Several Headsets

Every MindWave has the same Bluetooth name.
In Settings > Device > Scan, each row shows the
headset's address too: the one in use is filled
and the connected one shows a Bluetooth sign.
Tap the pencil on a row to name the headset;
the Session screen and new sessions use the
name. Switching headsets waits until the
session stops.

## Sensor Contact

For good EEG signal:
- Wash skin in sensor area (forehead, earlobe)
  with soap to remove oil, sweat, and dead skin
- Wipe sensor pads with alcohol
- Press sensor firmly against dry, clean skin
- Minimize hair under the sensor
- Signal quality shown during connection
  (0 = perfect, 200 = no contact)

## Audio Feedback

Two feedback channels in Settings > Audio. Tap a
channel to open a picker; Custom file... picks your
own audio (wav/mp3/ogg/flac/m4a; on Android in the
system file picker, and the app keeps a copy):
- Feedback sound (below threshold): Off / Rain / Tone
  / Custom. Plays while you are below your threshold
  and fades out as you approach it. Default Rain.
- Reward sound (above threshold): Off / Rain / Tone /
  Crickets / Custom. Silent until you cross the
  threshold, then fades in and grows the further above
  you go. Default Off. Crickets is a soft cricket chirp
  about every 10 s (a "still here / doing well" cue).

So: quieter (or the reward swelling) = deeper. The
two cross-fade at the threshold. Set either to Off
for no sound on that side. Volume rises on a log
curve, max 0.3.

Alert sounds, each off until you enable it in
the Audio section above:
- Bell = sinking/dullness detected
- Chime = subtle distraction
- Warble = headset disconnected or signal lost
  (a session that stops because no data arrives
  warbles anyway)

The timer's end sound (Settings > Timer / Program) is picked
the same way: Default bell or Custom file...; Test
plays it. A file the app can't play rings the
default bell, and Test says so.

## Text Size

Settings > Display > Text size makes every
text larger: Normal, Large (125%) or Larger
(150%). Each profile keeps its own. Takes
effect immediately.

## Scored Line Colours

Settings > Display > Scored line colours sets
how the line you train on is coloured on the
graphs: Mono (its own colour) or a gradient by
its value from 0 to 200 (Blue › Red, Viridis,
Plasma, Turbo, Red › Green). Each profile keeps
its own. Takes effect immediately.

## History

Tap a day in the calendar (or the 14-day bars)
to list its sessions; tap it again or Show All
to list every session.

The arrows above the chart (or a sideways
swipe on it) show earlier or later days, back
to your first session; tap the dates between
them to return to today.

The table under the chart sums the day, its
week (Monday to Sunday) and its month - today's,
or the day you tapped - and all time:
- Total: how long you meditated
- On target: time at or above the threshold,
  each session against its own target
- Best streak: the longest run above it

The chevron next to the History title folds
the chart and the table away, so the list gets
the whole screen; tap it again to unfold.

The |<->| button in a graph's bottom-right
corner shows the whole session on it and the
graphs beside it (Raw EEG: its last minute);
tap it again to go back to the window you had.
In full screen the X in the top-right corner
closes it.

## Locked-Screen Sessions (Android)

You can start a session, lock the screen, and
EEG processing and audio feedback continue
uninterrupted. A persistent notification stays
visible while the session is active. If the
session stops unexpectedly, a warble sound
plays to alert you.

Some phones (Huawei, Xiaomi, Samsung and
others) close background apps anyway. At the
first start the app shows what to allow:
Battery (battery optimisation) and, on Huawei,
App launch (Settings > Battery > App launch):
Manage manually, with all three switches on.
The same buttons are in
Settings > Device.

## App Crashes

If the app hits an unexpected error, a crash dialog
will appear with the full report already copied to
your clipboard. Please paste it into a new GitHub
issue so we can fix it.

## Data Backup

Settings > Data Backup > tap "Backup database"
to write a copy of all profiles, sessions and
settings to a file. You choose where to save it
(on Android a system save dialog opens); the
suggested name holds the profile name, date and
time. Custom sound files are not included in a
backup.

Tap "Restore database" to replace the ENTIRE
database — all profiles on this device — with a
backup file. This cannot be undone. Restore is
unavailable while a session is running. The app
will exit after restoring; relaunch to see your
imported history.

## User Profiles

If you've used the app before and reinstall (or
move to a new device by restoring a backup), your
existing profiles are shown when you create a
profile. Tap an existing profile to keep using it,
or type a new name to start fresh. Names must be
unique — if you type a name that exists, you'll be
offered a "Use existing" / "Change name" choice.

You can't switch profiles while a session is
running or connecting — stop the session first.
Switching to another profile clears the last
session from the Session screen; it stays in
History.

## Session Program

Settings > Timer / Program > Simple | Program
toggle. "Enable Timer" is only shown in Simple
mode.

Program mode builds a sequence of timed
segments, each with its own metric, target,
end cue and feedback sound. At each boundary
the ending segment's end cue plays, and the
target and the sound switch to the next
segment's. The session stops when the last
segment ends, with the timer's end sound.

Each segment row: Min (its length), Metric
(Shamatha / Meditation / NS Attention / NS
Meditation / a saved custom formula), Target,
End cue (Chime or Warble), Feedback (Default /
Rain / Tone / Custom file...; Default uses the
Audio setting), and X to remove it.

Add rows with "+ Add Segment". Save the whole
program by name; re-saving under the same name
overwrites it. Loading another program asks
first only when the editor has unsaved changes;
deleting one always asks. The editor shows the
loaded program's name, and saved programs
appear in a list you can tap to load.

On the Session screen, the button next to Start
shows "P" in program mode. Tap it, then
Programs…, to quick-load a saved program; the
list highlights the loaded one.

During a program the graph shows each segment's
metric, and the legend marks the active one with
a bold "»". The live graph and the session
detail's graph show a stepped threshold line
that follows each segment's target.

A segment whose formula has an error uses
Shamatha, and a message at the session's start
names the formula.

## Try Another Threshold (session detail)

Open a session in History. Under its stats,
Try another threshold shows the time above
threshold and the longest streak at another
threshold: move the slider (or -/+) and both
are recalculated from the session's recorded
data; the graph's dashed line follows. Reset
goes back to the session as recorded. Nothing
is saved.

A program session has a slider per segment,
each with its own time above and streak; the
Whole session totals follow them. A streak
that runs on into the next segment counts as
one there, as it did live.

The chevron after the title folds the section
away; it stays folded for your profile.

## Custom Formulas

Settings > Custom Formula has three named slots.
Each slot (Formula 1/2/3) has a name field, an
expression input, Apply (evaluate) and Save (to
the library).

Save refuses a formula with an error and shows
the error under its slot. Saved Formulas below
the slots is your library: tap one to put it in
the first empty slot, or its X to delete it.
Export to .txt writes the library's formulas,
one per line, to Documents/EEGMeditation.

The expression is a Python-style formula. Available
variables include band powers (alpha1, alpha2,
beta1, beta2, theta, delta, gamma1, gamma2, and the
sums alpha, beta, gamma), normalized bands
(alpha_norm etc.), sqrt-normalized bands
(s_alpha1, s_beta2 etc.), computed scores
(shamatha_score, meditation_score, distraction,
sinking), and windowed avg(expr, N).

One slot can drive the feedback sound. Select it
with the [1][2][3] buttons next to the
"Custom Formula" option in Settings > Threshold.
If that slot's formula is invalid, audio falls back
to shamatha.

All three formula series share one Y-axis (0–200,
reference line at 100). Keep your formula output
in a range comparable to the other metrics so the
graph stays readable.

To assign a saved formula to a slot without opening
Settings, use the "Choose..." button on the matching
custom-formula row in the on-graph series picker
(the list icon in the top-left of the metrics graph).

A session's detail replays its own formulas:
opening a past session shows the names and series
that were active at that time.
